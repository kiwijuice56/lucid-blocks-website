about

lucid blocks is an open world survival sandbox by lucy b. locks with psychedelic and dream-like worlds to explore, available on Steam. some features include:

  • a unique crafting system (apotheosis) that lets you fuse together any set of items without needing to look up recipes.
  • an infinite world in all directions, with new environments and structures the higher or deeper you go.
  • powerful items like grappling hooks, bee gliders, ball wands, scuba diving gear, and explosives.
  • many lovely vermin to meet.

faq

  • future updates?

    yes, future content updates are planned! these will not be indefinite or regularly scheduled.

  • multiplayer?

    no, but there are multiple mods in progress.

  • translations?

    there are no official translations, but there exists fan-created patches:

    • russian/русский
    • simplified chinese/简体中文 and traditional chinese

  • controller support?

    yes, with rebinds available in the settings.

  • minimum specs?

    lucid blocks does not require a powerful pc, but you need a graphics device that supports Vulkan. search your device online to see if Vulkan is supported; you do not need to manually install Vulkan or any other software, but you may need to update your graphics drivers.

  • how was lucid blocks made?

    lucid blocks was created in the Godot game engine using C++ and GDscript. it started as a simple voxel engine to test Godot's capabilities, but evolved into its own project over time. many of the sprites are photos of junk from around my house or a dollar store. the art was made using Aseprite and the music was composed in FL Studio.
